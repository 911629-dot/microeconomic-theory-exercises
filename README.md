# Microeconomic Theory – Selected Exercises

This repository contains reformulated academic exercises in microeconomic theory,
at the master's level, with a focus on decision-making under risk and expected utility theory.

The material is representative of coursework typically covered in quantitative
economics programs such as QEM (Quantitative Economics / Models and Methods of Quantitative Economics).

Contents include:
- Expected utility representation (vNM)
- Certainty equivalents
- Risk aversion
- Comparison of lotteries

All exercises are reformulated versions of standard problems and are included
for academic practice and portfolio purposes.
